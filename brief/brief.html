YODHA: a DOOM agent that remembers, explores and finishes levels

Yugaantar 2026 · DevForge Track 1 "Can It Play DOOM?" · repo: github.com/AnshulMohanty/Agent_Yodha

USP

  • Spatial memory, built from rays. A two-scale egocentric semantic map (EgoMap) built from the 64 rays plus calibrated odometry. It remembers walls, doors, locked doors, keys, items, enemies, visited cells and the exploration frontier, so the agent can come back to a locked door with the key instead of looping.
  • Honest privileged teacher → DAgger → kickstarted PPO. A training-only expert sees the true map but only knows what the student has observed, so every decision is learnable from the student's own history. The student copies it on every visited state, then PPO improves on it with a scorer-aligned reward. Long-horizon behaviour (key → lock → exit) comes in millions of steps on a laptop, not billions.
  • Training maps in the organizers' own format. Procedural UDMF grid maps with exactly the template fixtures' door, lock and exit specials (Door_Raise 12, Door_LockedRaise 13 with locks 129–131, Exit_Normal 243 on GATE floors or switches), four tiers, plus real Freedoom 2 levels, so the agent generalises zero-shot instead of memorising.

Neural architecture (≈4.6M params, ≈5 ms per decision on 1 CPU thread)

  • Variable enemies: each entity is embedded with its type, then two learned queries ("threat", "goal") attend over the masked set.
  • Hierarchical control: the intent head is supervised by the teacher's intent. It conditions the action heads through FiLM, so behaviour is interpretable (glass-box videos show intent probabilities live).
  • USE timing is learned. The previous action is an input, because Doom fires USE on the button-down edge and re-pressing it reverses a moving door.

Spatial memory design (EgoMap)

  • Odometry from the agent's own signals only. Heading comes from a calibrated table over the last turn presses; Doom keyboard turning is deterministic, with special start-of-map keys. Position comes from a linear model of the ego velocity in vitals. Held-out drift: heading 1.0° after 500 steps, position 7.4% of path.
  • Writing: each ray marks free space up to its hit and a typed hit cell (wall / door / locked / enemy / item). Entities are placed from (distance, sin, cos). Enemies and projectiles decay; keys are cleared when a key flag flips.
  • Reading: heading-up crops, fine (64×64 @ 32u, ±1024u) and coarse (32×32 @ 128u, ±2048u, incrementally max-pooled), plus a locally computed frontier channel. The CNN reads the memory; no planning or search runs on it.
  • Cost: 0.7 ms per step in numpy, about 5 ms per decision in total on one thread (validator p99 ≈ 8 ms vs a 40 ms budget).

Training and exploration pipeline

  • Map generator (4 tiers): rooms, corridors, doors on 1-wide doorways, key/lock gates on articulation cells, with keys in side branches; monsters, items, ambushes, exit alcove or exit switch. Randomised textures, light and heights. Solvability is checked by BFS over (cell, keys). Doors are never placed next to locks, because a Doom door only opens to its lowest neighbouring ceiling.
  • Honest teacher (training only): BFS over seen cells, with priorities fight → low-health pickup → exit → key → nearest frontier. It presses USE once per door. Exits and keys are "known" only once they've been inside the 90° view.
  • Reward (privileged state, training only), mirroring the scorer:

  • GAE (γ 0.995, λ 0.95), TBPTT over 32 steps, return-normalised reward, asymmetric critic (privileged features), auxiliary losses, and a tier curriculum that favours 20–80% success. Real Freedoom 2 maps are mixed into the RL phases.
  • Exploration drivers: new-sector and new-cell rewards, the frontier channel, self-history inputs (visits here, stuck counter, steps since a new cell), and anti-stuck penalties.
  • Compute: MacBook Air M5. Shared-memory workers (≈500 env steps/s), learner on the Apple GPU, plus a second run on a Kaggle T4.

Rules compliance, limitations, next steps

  • Compliance:
    • Actions are argmax/sampling over YodhaNet outputs. tests/test_audit.py forbids planners, atan2 and training imports in submission/.
    • The teacher, BFS and privileged reward live only in training/ ("training-time scaffolding", RULES §1–2).
    • No SB3. Evaluation maps are never accessed.
  • Limitations:
    • Odometry drifts on long runs and after collisions or knock-back. The EgoMap is self-consistent locally but not globally exact.
    • Training maps are axis-aligned grids; Freedoom levels add some irregular geometry.
    • The teacher is weak in heavy tier-4 fights, so tier-4 combat depends on PPO.
  • Next steps: scan-matching to correct drift, a longer PPO phase, a learned map-write module (differentiable EgoMap), and a recorded-demo curriculum on harder hand-made maps.
